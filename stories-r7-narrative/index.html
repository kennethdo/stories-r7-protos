硬規則 · 層級 · 設計拉文 · 提示入文 · spoon-feed · M1Hard rule · hierarchy · design pull · tip→article · spoon-feed · M1

1 · 層級：入 Story → 先見 story + crowd 互動 → 之後先去讀 Forbes。首屏主職係 story + crowd，唔係逼一入嚟就讀文。1 · Hierarchy: land on Story → see story + crowd interaction → then go read Forbes. First-screen job is story + crowd, not forcing article-first.

2 · 永遠可用：「讀文章」喺 call 前同 call 後都在——實心掣或者清楚嘅 Forbes ↗（帶 UTM），唔可以閘喺 call 後面。子域名／首頁／非文章 widget 入場一樣要有。2 · Always on: Read article stays available pre- and post-call — solid or clear Forbes ↗ with UTM; never gated behind the call. Same for subdomain / homepage / non-article widget entry.

3 · 設計拉文（唔係 copy-only）：摘錄／片段／引用／翻卡／追落去詞，用途係製造好奇 → 拉入 Forbes 全文。唔可以淨係「似新聞」或者淨加一句「去讀文」。成個 layout、節奏、揭示都要服務 immersion。3 · Design pull (not copy-only): excerpts / clips / quotes / flip / chase-words exist to create curiosity → pull into the Forbes full article. Not enough to look like news or bolt on a Read CTA. Layout, pacing, and reveal all serve immersion.

4 · 提示卡＝搵 tip 入文（side-track／extra）：來自 side-track／extra 嘅提示卡，唔係喺產品入面派完 tip。路徑：提示（side-track／extra）→ 搵 tip 時製造好奇 → Forbes 原文。撳提示＝想入文；答案喺原文，唔喺卡面派晒。4 · Hints = tip-seeking into the article (side-track/extra): hint cards from side-track/extra must not hand out the tip in-product. Path: hints (side-track/extra) → curiosity while seeking tips → Forbes article. Tapping a hint should make you want the article; the tip lives in the piece, not fully on the card.

驗收：如果拎走所有「讀文」字樣，設計仲會唔會令人想撳入 Forbes？會＝及格；淨靠文案＝不及格。提示卡：拎走卡面答案之後，搵 tip 仲會唔會想撳入文？Test: if every Read label were removed, would the design still make someone want Forbes? Yes = pass; copy-only = fail. Hint cards: if the tip answer were stripped from the card, would tip-seeking still pull into the article?

5 · 手機 spoon-feed（James 式 · 產品 pattern）：當幾個掣並排競爭（counter-actions），唔好淨係並列。用 tooltip／指引尖住主動作，短句鼓勵撳——例如「撳呢度睇 tip · 答案喺 Forbes」。呢個係互動 pattern，唔係淨加一句文案。5 · Mobile spoon-feed (James-style · product pattern): when multiple buttons compete (counter-actions), do not leave them as equals. Point a tooltip at the primary with a short nudge to click — e.g. “Tap here for the tip · it lives on Forbes.” This is an interaction pattern, not copy alone.
